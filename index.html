A SMALL MAP OF A BIG IDEA
FEATURE8 MIN READ

Neural networks are not mysterious. They are very good at adjusting.

The phrase “neural network” makes the subject sound biological and exotic. The useful truth is much less mysterious: a network is a pile of adjustable numbers that gets better by making mistakes.

That sounds almost too ordinary. But once you see the loop — predict, measure the mistake, adjust, repeat — a surprising amount of modern AI becomes easier to reason about.

WHY LIO EXISTS

Most technical writing starts with what the writer knows. We start with what the reader doesn't.

Good explanations have a human rhythm. They begin with a question, admit what is confusing, build a mental model, and only then introduce the vocabulary.

Lio keeps that rhythm while connecting every story to prerequisites, practice, and the next useful idea. The goal is not to make you read more. It is to make the next thing you learn easier.
